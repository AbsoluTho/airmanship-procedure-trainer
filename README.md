# Airmanship Procedure Trainer

An unofficial A320 **procedure** trainer that runs in the browser (iPad first). It is not a flight simulator: the aircraft will follow a fixed trajectory, and the training value is in the cockpit procedures, the MCDU and, later, the ND, PFD and FMA.

Not affiliated with any airline. Not a substitute for the FCOM, FCTM or operator manuals. The navigation data was typed from charts for training only and must never be used for navigation.

## Status: Phase 2 prototype (v0.11.1)

- **MCDU** (Thales FMS2 layout, per FCOM DSC-22): keyboard and scratchpad, INIT A, F-PLN with TMPY (ERASE/INSERT), LAT REV, DEPARTURE, AIRWAYS (with airway-to-airway intersection), ARRIVAL, waypoint insertion, CLR of waypoints and discontinuities.
- **Exercise** OEJN to OETB: RWY 16C MIGD2E, L677 WEJ, V54 TBK, ILS 31 via D179O, ALTN OEMA, FL340, CI 20, with automatic progress checks.
- **EFIS control panel and ND** shown as panel photos with live overlays: EFIS above the ND on the left, MCDU on the right. The EFIS option pushbuttons (CSTR, WPT, VOR D, NDB, ARPT) work one at a time: pressing one deselects the others, pressing the lit one turns it off. They light up and the mode and range knobs turn (tap a position or a knob half). The ND opens like the aircraft on the ground: ARC, 10 NM, GPS PRIMARY (cleared with CLR on the MCDU).
- **ND** (ROSE LS, ROSE VOR, ROSE NAV, ARC, PLAN; range 10 to 320 NM; CSTR, WPT, ARPT options; ROSE LS and ROSE VOR show the compass only for now): active F-PLN in green, TMPY in yellow dashed, missed approach in blue, alternate route in blue dashed, DME arcs and holds drawn. PLAN centres on the waypoint on the second line of the MCDU F-PLN page. Also live on both NDs in the 3D cockpit (EXT PWR on).
- **INIT B**: TAXI, ZFW/ZFWCG, BLOCK, TRIP/TIME, RTE RSV, ALTN, FINAL, EXTRA, TOW/LW, TRIP WIND, MIN DEST FOB. **F-PLN** now shows TIME predictions and EFOB at destination once ZFW and BLOCK are entered.
- **PERF TAKE OFF**: V1/VR/V2 (with V1/VR/V2 DISAGREE), F/S/O speeds, RWY, TO SHIFT, FLAPS/THS, FLEX, TRANS ALT, THR RED/ACC, ENG OUT ACC. Changing the takeoff runway clears the V speeds (CHECK TAKE OFF DATA).
- **Flight set-up save** (v0.11.1): SAVE on the flight controls (enabled once START FLIGHT is possible) keeps the MCDU data (INIT A and B, F-PLN, SEC F-PLN, PERF pages, RAD NAV, winds, FIX INFO, steps), the FCU and the EFIS settings in this browser; LOAD puts them back after a flight or in a later session, IRS aligned, ready for START. One set-up is kept; SAVE replaces it.
- **FMGC batch 3** (v0.11.0):
  - **Go-around**: TOGA on the flight controls (approach phase, in the air): MAN TOGA, SRS (go-around speed), GA TRK with NAV armed (engages above 100 ft), GA phase, flaps one step up; LVR CLB at the PERF GO AROUND THR RED altitude, CLB at its ACC altitude to the FCU altitude, green dot with flaps retracted on speed and gear up. LOC and G/S are lost (APPR to be armed again).
  - **Missed approach sequencing**: at the go-around the missed approach becomes the active route from the go-around point (T-P), shown green; the heading leg is flown to its altitude, then the waypoints and the published hold, flown as a hold (IMM EXIT). The approach is strung again after the missed approach, behind a discontinuity. ACTIVATE APPR PHASE (CONFIRM) on PERF GO AROUND starts the approach phase for a second approach and landing.
  - **Pseudo waypoints on the F-PLN**: (T/C), (T/D) and (DECEL) lines with time (UTC in flight) and altitude. The approach phase is activated at DECEL (25 NM before the threshold, magenta D on the ND) when the aircraft is in NAV or LOC; otherwise with ACTIVATE APPR PHASE.
  - **RAD NAV**: the LS autotuned from the approach shows its frequency (OETB ILS 31 IPKS 111.90; check against the current chart).
- **FMGC batch 2** (v0.10.0), page layouts from the FlyByWire A32NX MCDU:
  - **SEC INIT A, SEC INIT FUEL PRED and SEC PERF** (SEC INDEX INIT> and PERF>): the SEC F-PLN keeps its own INIT A, INIT B, PERF, speed limit and wind data (copied with COPY ACTIVE); ACTIVATE SEC makes them the active data, SWAP ACTIVE exchanges them.
  - **STEP ALTS** (PERF CRZ STEP ALTS> before the descent, VERT REV STEP ALTS> at en-route waypoints): up to four steps entered as `FL360/LABUN`, edited (altitude, `/WPT`) or cleared with CLR; steps of 1000 ft at least, no step climb after a step descent; DIST and TIME to each step, STEP AHEAD within 20 NM, ABOVE MAX and the STEP ABOVE MAX FL message above REC MAX (EO MAX with one engine). S/C or S/D on the F-PLN at the step waypoint, step climb (cyan) or step descent (white) symbol on the ND. In flight the step starts at the step waypoint (or after it) once the FCU altitude is set to the step altitude: a step climb in CLB, a step descent as a cruise descent in DES at 1000 ft/min; the new level becomes the CRZ FL. STEP AHEAD message 20 NM before.
  - **Engine out**: ENG 1 or ENG 2 failure from the flight controls (in flight). The failed engine windmills on the E/WD and the SD pages follow (HYD: engine pump LO and PTU pressurising that system; ELEC: GEN amber, bus tie from the other generator; BLEED: valves closed, amber ducts). The climb capability drops; the FMGC sets the EO condition: EO CLR* (amber) on PERF CLB, CRZ and DES, managed speed green dot in climb and cruise (EO shown on the PERF pages), EO MAX on PROG in place of REC MAX. EO CLR* clears the FMGC condition only.
  - **PROG**: VDEV (from the managed descent path) in the descent and approach.
  - **RTA** (VERT REV RTA>): AT WPT, RTA (hhmm or hhmmss UTC), DIST, ETA, TIME ERROR (early, late, on time), VMAX and the REQUIRED SPD; in cruise the managed Mach is adjusted, between green dot and M.80, to meet the RTA.
- **FMGC batch 1** (v0.9.0), page layouts from the FlyByWire A32NX MCDU:
  - **LAT REV** laid out as FlyByWire: DEPARTURE, ARRIVAL, FIX INFO (origin, and the FROM waypoint in flight), OFFSET (FROM waypoint in flight), NEXT WPT, HOLD, ENABLE ALTN, NEW DEST, AIRWAYS; PPOS (T-P or the runway climb leg) can be revised when it is the FROM line.
  - **NEW DEST** (LAT REV R4): in a temporary F-PLN, the route after the revised waypoint is deleted, then a discontinuity and the new destination (as FlyByWire); INIT A, the F-PLN, predictions and the flight follow the new destination, and its arrival is chosen with LAT REV at the destination.
  - **HOLD** (LAT REV <HOLD, HOLD AT page): COMPUTED hold (inbound course of the leg to the fix, right turns, 1.0 min up to 14000 ft, 1.5 min above) or DATABASE hold (the holds of the published missed approaches); INB CRS, TURN, TIME/DIST (time or distance), REVERT TO, LAST EXIT UTC and fuel in flight; in a temporary F-PLN until INSERT. F-PLN hold line `HOLD R` with the inbound course and the hold speed; ND racetrack. In flight the aircraft flies the racetrack circuit after circuit, decelerating to the hold speed (about green dot, 230 kt maximum up to 14000 ft) in managed speed; IMM EXIT* (amber, right key on the hold line) leaves at the next passage over the fix, RESUME HOLD* cancels it. CLR on the hold line deletes it.
  - **OFFSET** (LAT REV L2 at the FROM waypoint in flight): L1 to L50 or R1 to R50 (also 10R), CLR cancels; temporary F-PLN until INSERT. The aircraft joins the offset track at 45°, flies parallel to the route and rejoins at 45° before the first approach leg, where the offset ends. ND: offset path solid, original route dashed; `OFST R10` on the F-PLN title line. DIR TO cancels the offset.
  - **FIX INFO** 1/4 to 4/4 (arrows change the page): REF FIX (waypoint, navaid or airport), two radials and a radius, with the time (UTC in flight) and distance where the route first crosses each of them ahead of the aircraft; drawn on the ND as cyan dashed radials and circle (FlyByWire FixInfoLayer).
  - **Scratchpad messages**: entry errors (type I) are shown over the scratchpad, which keeps its text; system messages (type II, white or amber) are listed and shown while the scratchpad is empty, amber first, removed by CLR or when resolved: GPS PRIMARY, CHECK TAKE OFF DATA, V1/VR/V2 DISAGREE, T/D REACHED, ENTER DEST DATA (180 NM before the destination without QNH, TEMP and wind on PERF APPR), DEST EFOB BELOW MIN, SET HOLD SPEED, and TEMPORARY F-PLN EXISTS (DIR TO with a temporary F-PLN).
- **Altitude and speed constraints** (v0.8.0): managed CLB levels off at an AT or AT-OR-BELOW constraint (ALT CST\*, ALT CST, with CLB armed) and climbs again once the waypoint is sequenced; managed DES follows a 3° path built back from the runway that meets every arrival constraint (level segments flown in DES) and does not go below an AT or AT-OR-ABOVE constraint before its waypoint; speed constraints cap the managed speed (climb: up to the waypoint; descent: from the waypoint on, decelerating before it); T/D moves with the constraints. OP CLB, OP DES, V/S and FPA ignore them. ALT armed and the PFD target altitude turn magenta when the next level-off is a constraint. ND: constraint circles in flight (magenta predicted met, amber missed, white not taken into account), magenta level-off and start of climb/descent symbols, all vertical-profile arrows drawn with the tip on the route. F-PLN: constrained waypoints show the predicted altitude after a magenta (met) or amber (missed) star.
- **OPEN CLB / OPEN DES and speed** (v0.8.0), after the Airbus AP/FD and A/THR integration logic: OP CLB, CLB and EXP CLB fly CLB thrust with the speed on the elevator, and OP DES and EXP DES fly idle thrust with the speed on the elevator, so the vertical speed is what the thrust leaves after paying for any acceleration (it shallows while accelerating, steepens while slowing in the climb, and depends on altitude, weight, speed, flaps and gear). For a level change under 1200 ft, OP CLB and OP DES fly 1000 ft/min with A/THR on speed. Managed and selected speed switch from SPD to MACH in the climb when the speed reaches the programmed Mach, and from MACH to SPD in the descent at the programmed descent speed (the FCU window and FMA follow); EXP DES targets 340 kt / M.80. The PFD shows SPEED or MACH with the target above the speed tape, a speed trend arrow (also on the takeoff and landing rolls), and a larger target altitude, and the managed descent path as a solid green dot on the altitude tape. Takeoff acceleration and landing deceleration are higher (about 32 s to VR with FLEX).
- **ECAM** (v0.8.0), behind the MCDU: the button at the top centre of the MCDU switches between MCDU and ECAM (same place in both views). The upper (E/WD) and lower (SD) displays are drawn in reference display-unit photos and together take the MCDU's place. With the ECAM shown, the ECAM control panel (photo) appears over the lower half of the ND and disappears when going back to the MCDU; the ECP button in the ND's lower right corner shows or hides the panel in either view. E/WD: N1 dials (thrust lever position, N1 limit), EGT dials, N2, FF, thrust limit (FLX/TOGA/CLB) with the N1 limit, FOB, slats/flaps with the configuration, T.O memo (T.O CONFIG test from the T.O CONFIG key), LDG memo below 2000 ft, T.O INHIBIT, LDG INHIBIT, PARK BRK. SD: automatic page by flight phase (WHEEL on the ground, ENGINE for takeoff, CRUISE, WHEEL with the gear down), the ENG, BLEED, PRESS, ELEC, HYD, FUEL, APU, COND, DOOR, WHEEL, F/CTL, STS and ALL keys (the selected key lights; press it again, CLR or RCL for the automatic page), all thirteen pages ported from the FlyByWire A32NX SD pages (same coordinates, symbols and text sizes, drawn as SVG over the screen): ENGINE, CRUISE, WHEEL, FUEL, DOOR/OXY, F/CTL, BLEED, CAB PRESS, ELEC, HYD, APU, COND and STATUS; permanent data TAT, SAT, UTC, GW. Pages whose drawing reaches below the permanent-data line (FUEL, BLEED, CAB PRESS, HYD, APU) are scaled 1 to 2 % to fit. Live from the model: engine N2 (HP valves, engine-driven pumps), cabin altitude, V/S and ΔP (CAB PRESS gauges, outflow valve), ground or flight (GND air, vent INLET/OUTLET), and on the ground the EXT PWR and APU MASTER SW: EXT PWR box on ELEC, APU GEN box, APU AVAIL and FLAP OPEN, APU bleed open with the crossbleed open and the engine bleed valves closed. The engine generators stay on line (the engines run at idle on the ECAM), so EXT PWR and APU GEN show as available but not supplying. Systems that are not simulated are shown in their normal state (packs on, pumps on, PTU and RAT standby, batteries on in flight, APU off in flight, STATUS NORMAL).
- **App icon and install** (v0.7.1): browser tab icon, iPhone/iPad home-screen icon, Android/desktop install with a web app manifest (`manifest.webmanifest`, `icons/`). On the iPad: open the site in Safari, Share, Add to Home Screen.
- **v0.7.0 from the first flight notes**: DIR TO page (in flight, from T-P, temporary F-PLN until INSERT, NAV engages); alternate F-PLN after END OF F-PLN in blue (destination, DCT, alternate), ALTN runway via LAT REV ARRIVAL, ALTN entry on LAT REV at the destination and ENABLE ALTN at a waypoint (FlyByWire); the F-PLN restarts at the FROM line when a waypoint is sequenced, TO waypoint in white, DEST distance counts down; the flight path is rebuilt when the active F-PLN changes in flight; PERF CRZ shows T/D distance and time; deceleration (since v0.10.0: 12 NM flaps 1, 8 NM flaps 2, 6 NM gear down, flaps 3 five seconds later, flaps FULL three seconds after that); ND T/C or level-off (cyan) and T/D (white) symbols and DECEL (D); VOR/ADF switches on the EFIS with ND bearing pointers and VOR1/VOR2 data (manual RAD NAV or nearest station autotuned); EFIS QNH knob with QNH/STD and in Hg/hPa, altitude corrected for the setting, flashing at the transition altitude and level; PFD: bigger FMA, S/F speeds drawn in front, target altitude kept behind the readout window, green vertical deviation dot on the altitude tape in descent; the SPD knob starts from the present speed or Mach.
- **FCU** (photo with live 7-segment displays in the FlyByWire AirbusFCU font): SPD/MACH, HDG/TRK, ALT and V/S/FPA windows with managed dots and dashes and the LVL/CH dot; knobs turn, pull (selected) and push (managed) through a touch pad; AP1, AP2, A/THR, LOC, APPR and EXPED pushbuttons light up; SPD/MACH, HDG-V/S / TRK-FPA, METRIC ALT and the 100/1000 ALT step work.
- **Guidance from the FCU and FMGS**: lateral RWY, RWY TRK, NAV (re-engages only when the route is intercepted), HDG/TRK, LOC* and LOC; vertical SRS, CLB, OP CLB, EXP CLB, DES (armed before T/D, engages at T/D), OP DES, EXP DES, V/S, FPA, ALT* and ALT (holds the captured altitude until the ALT knob is pulled or pushed), ALT CRZ, G/S* and G/S, LAND, FLARE, ROLL OUT; managed or selected speed; T/D REACHED when the FCU altitude was not lowered. CAT 3 DUAL with AP1+2, SINGLE with one AP, CAT 1 with FD only.
- **EFIS FD and LS keys**: FD shows or removes the FD bars and sets 1 FD 2 / - FD 2 on the FMA; LS shows the LOC and G/S scales with the ILS ident and distance, and LS flashes amber when APPR or LOC is armed without LS. TRK/FPA shows the flight path vector.
- **Layout**: EFIS control panel and FCU aligned on the glareshield row, PFD and ND below, the whole deck matching the MCDU height; flight controls in a bar underneath.
- **Automatic flight (Phase 2)**, captain's side only: START FLIGHT once there is an active F-PLN with a departure runway, INIT A (FROM/TO, FLT NBR, COST INDEX, CRZ FL), INIT B (ZFW/ZFWCG, BLOCK), PERF TAKE OFF (V1, VR, V2, FLAPS/THS) and an FCU altitude above the airfield; an approach is not required to start (without an ILS approach the aircraft cannot autoland). The aircraft flies the active F-PLN on a fixed path and a scripted profile: FLX takeoff, THR RED and ACC from PERF, 250 kt below 10000 ft, ECON climb and Mach cruise at the CRZ FL, descent from T/D on a 3° profile, level at the FAF altitude, LOC and G/S capture, CAT 3 DUAL autoland, roll-out and stop. Pause, speed ×1 (at start) to ×64, and skip to T/D or the approach.
- **PFD** drawn to match the reference A320 PFD photo (layout, colours, attitude shape, tapes, readout window), lined up with the ND, flight controls above it: FMA (five columns, new modes boxed for 10 s), attitude and roll scale, FD bars, speed tape (VLS, VMAX, S/F/green dot, V1, target), Mach, altitude tape with target altitude and STD/QNH, vertical speed, heading tape, radio altitude, LOC and G/S deviation with the ILS ident and distance.
- **Live during the flight**: ND (aircraft position, GS/TAS, TO waypoint), F-PLN sequencing (passed waypoints disappear, UTC and EFOB), flight phases (PROG, PERF page titles, ACTIVATE APPR PHASE), fuel burn, and the captain's PFD and ND in the 3D cockpit.
- **Departures** start with a climb on runway track to 400 ft AAL when a SID is inserted, or to 1500 ft AAL for a runway-only (visual) departure, shown as an altitude leg such as `(426)` with `C156°`.
- **SEC F-PLN** is shown all in white and is revised directly with the normal F-PLN, LAT REV, DEPARTURE, ARRIVAL, AIRWAYS and VERT REV pages, with no temporary flight plan; it is drawn in white on the ND while displayed.
- **More MCDU/FMGC pages** (layouts from FlyByWire A32NX, figures from the simplified model):
  - IRS INIT (from INIT A; simulated 90 s alignment, ALIGN ON REF), CLIMB/CRZ/DES WIND (from INIT A or VERT REV)
  - RAD NAV (VOR, ADF tuned by ident or frequency from OurAirports; LS autotuned from the selected approach; the ILS frequency is not in the data yet)
  - VERT REV (CLB/DES SPD LIM, SPD and ALT constraints); the F-PLN right keys take SPD/ALT entries (for example `250/`, `/+5000`, `/FL100`) or open VERT REV; CLR deletes constraints
  - PERF CLB, CRZ, DES, APPR (QNH, TEMP, MAG WIND, TRANS ALT, BARO/RADIO minimum, VAPP/VLS, LDG CONF) and GO AROUND, linked by PREV/NEXT PHASE; ACTIVATE APPR PHASE appears once the flight phases exist
  - SEC INDEX (COPY ACTIVE, SEC F-PLN, DELETE, ACTIVATE, SWAP), FUEL PRED, PROG (CRZ/OPT/REC MAX, BRG/DIST TO, REPORT, PREDICTIVE GPS)
  - DATA INDEX 1/2 and 2/2: position, IRS and GPS monitors, A/C STATUS, CLOSEST AIRPORTS, waypoint, navaid and runway lookup
  - AIRPORT key cycles through origin, destination and alternate on the F-PLN
  - The PERF key opens the page of the current flight phase. Live in-flight behaviour comes with the automatic flight of a later phase.
- **ND PLAN** uses the whole screen for the map, so the route runs past the range circle.
- **Cockpit 3D** (Three.js): cockpit panels as textured planes, touch look and zoom, view presets; BAT 1, BAT 2, EXT PWR and APU MASTER SW clickable; live MCDU screens on the pedestal (powered by EXT PWR).

## Data and references

- **OurAirports** (public domain, https://ourairports.com/data/): 37 Saudi airports with runway thresholds, lengths and elevations, and 54 navaids (VOR, VORTAC, DME, NDB) with frequencies. Rebuild with `python3 tools/navdb/ourairports.py` (downloads the CSV files into `tools/navdb/ourairports/`, which is not committed). Airports typed from charts keep their chart elevation, variation and runway courses; their threshold positions now come from OurAirports. Any of these airports can be used in INIT A (FROM/TO, ALTN); only OEJN and OETB have procedures.
- **FlyByWire A32NX** (GPL-3.0, https://github.com/flybywiresim/aircraft): the AirbusFCU font (`fonts/AirbusFCU.ttf`), FCU display formats and FMA mode names; the system display pages ENGINE, CRUISE, WHEEL, FUEL, DOOR/OXY, F/CTL, BLEED, CAB PRESS, ELEC, HYD, APU, COND and STATUS (`js/sdpages.js`, ported from their SD and SDv2 pages, with the gauge parts from their common gauges); reference for the LAT REV, HOLD AT, FIX INFO, STEP ALTS, RTA, SEC INIT/PERF, PROG (VDEV, EO CLR*), INIT FUEL PRED and PERF TAKE OFF page layouts, the F-PLN hold line, NEW DEST and the system message texts and for the ND ROSE and PLAN range labels and compass points. This project is GPL-3.0 as well.
- **FAA CIFP** covers United States procedures only, so it is not used for the Saudi exercises. It could feed a future US exercise route through an ARINC 424 parser.

## Performance figures

`js/perf.js` is a generic, simplified A320-class model (climb, cruise and descent blocks, weight factor, rule-of-thumb F, S and green dot speeds). It exists so the pages show plausible numbers. It is not the FMGS performance database or a performance tool, and its figures must never be used for real flight planning. Transition altitudes in `js/data/procedures.js` default to 13000 ft and should be checked against the current AIP.
