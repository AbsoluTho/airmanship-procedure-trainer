      How to

      Type with the MCDU keys or your keyboard. CLR on an empty scratchpad writes CLR: then press the LSK of a waypoint or discontinuity to delete it. Left LSK next to a waypoint: LAT REV (DEPARTURE, NEXT WPT, AIRWAYS, ARRIVAL). Up and down arrows scroll the F-PLN. INIT, then the right arrow key: INIT B (ZFW/ZFWCG, BLOCK). PERF: TAKE OFF page (V1, VR, V2, FLAPS/THS, FLEX). EFIS control panel: tap a pushbutton, or a knob position (or the left or right half of a knob) to turn it. ND in PLAN: the map centres on the waypoint on the second line of the F-PLN page, so scroll the F-PLN to review the route. CLR clears GPS PRIMARY from the MCDU and the ND. FCU: tap a knob for TURN, PULL (selected) and PUSH (managed). Set the initial clearance altitude before takeoff (example 7000 ft). Flight: START FLIGHT in the bar under the displays. After takeoff engage AP1, set FL340 and push ALT for managed climb. Descent: before T/D set the FCU altitude (example 4200 ft) and push ALT. About 25 NM out press LS, APPR and AP2 for CAT 3 DUAL. NAV only re-engages when the aircraft intercepts the route, so set an intercept heading before pushing the HDG knob.
      Drag to look · pinch or scroll to zoom · tap BAT 1, BAT 2, EXT PWR, APU MASTER or an MCDU
