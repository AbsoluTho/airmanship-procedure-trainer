These recordings are licensed separately from the trainer's GPL-licensed code. Commercial use requires separate permission from the relevant rights holders.

The source folder is [`sound/Originals/SFX`](https://github.com/flybywiresim/a32nx-wwise/tree/master/sound/Originals/SFX). The source repository's [credits](https://github.com/flybywiresim/a32nx-wwise/blob/master/sound/credits.txt) name Boris for the cabin-call sounds and JayZee#2201 for CRC and Master Caution recordings. Thanks to the FlyByWire sound contributors. No FlyByWire endorsement is implied.
